- **Draft:** 9 of `docs/agent-prompt.md`. Drafts 10 and 11 merged while this ran. Draft 10 changed "N still open" and "reply with the number". Draft 11 gives each list its own page and sends a link in place of the list.
- **Where:** two Claude agents per chat. One played the person from `docs/personas.md`, the other played Handup from the system prompt. Neither could see the other's instructions. Not the Worker and not WhatsApp.
- **Who chatted:** five made-up personas, A to E. Nobody here is real.
- **Kind of run:** free chats, 8 to 11 messages each
- **Marked by:** Claude, working with Samira
- **Result:** no safety or money rule was broken in any chat. Four of five ended with a message they said they would send. All five were put off by the opening.

**How much to trust this.** These are agents acting out moods we wrote down, including what would put each person off. So they are good at showing where the prompt can't bend, and no proof of how real people feel. One real person still beats all five.
